A tower defense game made in Unity.

The player doesn't control any character diretly, but only has the ability to
guide a gaint monster by strategically placing pieces of food.

The game was interesting in concept but flawed in execution as players are
unable to have a strong direct impact on the easily distracted monster. 

It features some lovely Egytian inspired music by my then 15 year old sister
Sylvia Roberts.
